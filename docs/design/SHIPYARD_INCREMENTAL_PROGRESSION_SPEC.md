# Shipyard Incremental Progression Spec

## Purpose

The shipyard converts home production and expedition rewards into permanent player power.

## Upgrade tracks

- Repair Bay
- Module Printer
- Blueprint Archive
- Drydock Expansion
- Drone Automation
- Research Lab
- Solar Power Link
- Subspace Launch Array

## Design rules

- early upgrades require safe home-produced materials;
- mid-tier upgrades require expedition salvage and research data;
- advanced upgrades require anomaly/exotic materials;
- build capacity increases slowly and visibly;
- the shipyard should be the bridge between home automation and expedition power.
